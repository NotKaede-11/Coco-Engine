## Rating and uncertainty

**Release estimate: approximately 2,977 Elo**, from a filtered Ordo fit over **4442 games** (1971W / 1727L / 744D, **52.75%**). Both games were removed from each of the 29 opening pairs containing a timeout or abandoned game, regardless of which engine lost. The 500 games against the substituted-network Coco 1.4 opponent are also excluded. The original games remain intact.

This is a **local, conditional estimate**, not an official CCRL rating. The fixed CCRL Blitz references are Lynx 1.5.0 = 2819, Sable 1.1.0 = 2933 and Stash 25.0 = 2932. A 1000-sample bootstrap of the remaining opponent/FEN clusters gives a conditional 95% percentile interval of **2959.3–2995.3** (seed 20260914). External-rating uncertainty, transfer to other conditions and systematic run defects are outside that interval. Excluding terminations after the run can introduce selection bias; filtering does not prove the rest of the run was free of contention.

## Test identity and conditions

- Candidate in the runner: `Coco-1.5.0-Dev`, executable SHA-256 `2567ff9222bf84026ec844bde424d99795877125880fb51b5829d942f36d9b83`. The match used the development executable; a complete source-to-binary reproduction was not established by this audit.
- Candidate external NNUE: 789508 bytes, SHA-256 `392be46c8e06c6d0cb6bedf00d8e3d08950d11daa883362de98f0c1deee68055`.
- Runner: fastchess alpha 1.8.1, `01010101-3de44228`; SHA-256 `f3aaab5109faf85a4dcfbe1b23bf2e97d6cafe43f8e9be8bad28511f07fe4f0c`.
- Time control: `10+0.1`, Coco Threads=1 / Hash=16 MiB, eight concurrent games, two colours, 250 rounds. Opponent-specific UCI settings are retained in the saved config; not every opponent exposes Threads.
- Seed: 5250043844425994889. Opening file: `noob_4moves.epd`; SHA-256 `4d836bffa2bf698bd8f38584ed865288f7a27e3c28cbe7767d1d45a8af7564ae`. Resign, draw, max-move and tablebase adjudication were disabled.
- **Only 25 distinct FENs per opponent** appeared in the actual PGNs. Each was repeated ten times as a colour pair (20 games). Thus 250 pairs per opponent do not mean 250 independent starting positions. The bootstrap groups all repeats of the same opponent/FEN together.
- Opponent executable hashes identify the files tested. Apart from the Coco historical executable digest check, their correspondence to official release downloads was not independently authenticated by this audit. A `coco.nnue` file present in an opponent working directory does not establish that that engine loaded it.

## Reliability

The PGNs record **4967 normal games, 32 time forfeits and one abandoned game**. Coco lost 11 games on time (0.22% of its 5000 games). Opponent time losses were Lynx 10, Noggin 5, Potential 3, Stash 2 and Sable 1. The abandoned game was a Lynx connection stall, scored as a Coco win. No PGN termination is labelled an illegal-move loss; the full move text was not independently replayed through a second chess library.

Coco 1.4 played only 500 games; the development engine played 5000. Both had zero timeouts in their shared 500-game matchup. Those games were also shorter: median 43 plies, versus 124 plies against the other opponents. Shorter games provide fewer opportunities for prolonged low-clock play. These observations do not demonstrate that Coco 1.4 has better time management, particularly with its substituted network. The development engine's 11 time losses still warrant a separate controlled timing investigation.

Coco emitted 3547 warnings for PV continuation beyond threefold repetition and 828 beyond the fifty-move rule. These are displayed-line warnings, not 4375 illegal game moves. No Coco bestmove/PV-start mismatch was found in the warning tally. Other engines also generated PV warnings.

Concurrency was eight on an i5-12450H Windows 11 laptop, with no runner time margin. Other CPU workloads overlapped parts of the tournament. The time losses are real observations; their cause has not been isolated between engine timing and host scheduling. This report does not certify a clean, uncontended run. A lower-concurrency timing check and more distinct openings are recommended before making stronger release-strength claims.

## Historical comparison

The Coco 1.4 Windows executable matches its official release digest, but the tested external network was substituted (394756 bytes instead of the release's 789508-byte asset). The 500–0 score is retained in the raw table and excluded from rating calibration and historical-gain claims. See the [identity evidence](gauntlet-rating-method.md#historical-opponent-identity). Different networks between versions are legitimate; an unvalidated replacement is the issue here.
